---
title: Calendar
description: Appointments and task and project deadlines in OmniConsole.
---

Open **Calendar** in the Work sidebar to see your OmniOS appointments alongside task and project deadlines. Switch between month, week and day views, use Today to return to the current date, or filter by company and person.

## Appointments

Choose **New appointment** to create a meeting or reminder. Set its company, dates, participants and visibility. You can also include a location, meeting link, notes, reminders and recurrence.

Click an appointment to edit it, mark it complete or cancel it. The calendar saves to the same OmniOS agenda used by the web console. Personal appointments remain visible to their authorized owners and participants.

## Deadlines

Click a task deadline to open its task details over the calendar. Notes, steps and edits use the same task record as Tasks and Kanban.

Choose **Project deadline** to give an existing project a due date, or click a project deadline to change it. Clearing the date removes that deadline. Project dates and task dates are separate: changing the project date does not move its tasks.

## Availability

Calendar dates use your device’s timezone. Project deadlines are calendar dates and do not move when your timezone changes.

If a calendar layer cannot be read, the page shows its error. A hub version without project deadlines displays a specific availability message. The calendar refreshes while visible and after saved changes; switching accounts clears the previous person’s calendar and open editors.
